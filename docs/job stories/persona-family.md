### Context and Roles
* A family of 4 buying tickets for a summer trip
  - 2 adults
  - 1 child
  - 1 toddler

### Goals and Success
**Primary goal**: Find and purchase four flight tickets in a single booking with adjacent seats selected

### Pain Points
* Will need the ability to purchase multiple tickets at the same time without making repetitive bookings.
* All four tickets can be managed by one person
* Will need seat selection to take care of children during the flight
* Will go to another website if a booking system is too hard to navigate

### Constraints and Non-Goals
#### Constraints
* Limited tolerance for administrative actions at the airport ((children and toddlers needed to be taken care of)
* Low tolerance for delays (plans are set and booked)

#### Non-Goals
* Doesn’t want to learn about the booking system

---

## User Stories

### 1. Seat Selection Map
> **As an** adult of a family,
> I can request toddler seats and choose adjacent seats,
> so that my family can fly while seated with each other.

### 2. Deals and Promotions
> **As an** adult of a family,
> I can discover deals and destinations on promotion,
> so that it can help with deciding where to go and save money.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Criterion 1**: Given that the flight results are displayed, when I try to sort the flights, then I should be able to sort the results with the best prices.
* **Criterion 2**: Given that I am navigating the website, when I navigate to the deals/promotion page, then I should be able to easily find good deals and popular visiting places.

### 3. Manage Bookings
> **As a** customer travelling with a family,
> I can manage all the bookings together,
> so that fewer people and work are involved when making changes to the plans.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Criterion 1**: Given that I have booked multiple tickets, when I navigate to the manage page, then I should be able to view or make changes to all the tickets.
* **Criterion 2**: Given that I want to book multiple tickets, when I am searching for flights, then I should be able to select multiple passengers to easily book multiple tickets at once.
