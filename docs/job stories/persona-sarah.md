### Context and Roles
**Name**: Sarah
  * A busy mom of two children, trying to book a flight for a planned family holiday during the school winter holidays.
  * She has just opened the flight booking website and wants to quickly select and book a flight that matches her budget and schedule.

### Goals and Success
**Primary goals**: Find tickets for her and her family ensuing that the family sits together, the flight times align with her schedule, and the ability to easily manage multiple tickets.
  * She will be done with her goal when she successfully finds the flights and the seats that fit her criteria and manages to book them.
  * She will know she achieved her goal when she receives a confirmation email from the website for all her tickets with ticket IDs.

### Pain Points
* Must make sure the children sit next to one of the parents to ensure a stress-free flight experience.
* Worried about there not being early flight times – It will be very stressful if the flight departs or lands after the children’s bedtimes.
* Needs to make sure if she had booked the right seats and times.
* Will have to postpone the plans if an appropriate flight is not available

### Constraints and Non-Goals
#### Constraints
* Limited time to browse flights (As a busy mom)
* Has a restricted budget, so will avoid expensive flights and seats
* Very strict scheduling dates (Needs to get back before the holidays end, so cannot shift the planned flight dates by much)

#### Non-Goals
* Doesn’t care about gaining loyalty points
* Doesn’t care about signing up for promotional emails
* Not looking for luxury upgrades

## User Stories

### 1. Seat Selection Map
> **As a** customer travelling with kids, 
> I can select and assign seats for each member of my family,
> so that I can have a stress-free journey knowing that the children will be seated near one of the parents.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Criterion 1**: Given that I have selected my flight, when I select seats for each family member, then I should see a clear sign of the seat information that I have selected
* **Criterion 2**: Given that I have selected the seats, when I have finished booking the tickets, then the seat numbers should be clearly displayed in the tickets.

### 2. Flight Filtering Option
> **As a** customer travelling with kids,
> I can filter flights by specific departure/arrival times,
> so that I can avoid booking on times that may disrupt my kids’ sleep schedules and lead to a stressful time at the airport.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Criterion 1**: Given that the flight results are displayed when I select a departure time in the filter, then the system should update the results to only show flights available at or after the specified time.
* **Criterion 2**: Given that I have selected multiple filtering options, when I want to clear the filters, then there should be a clear way to do so within the system.

### 3. Age Selection Dropdown
> **As a** customer travelling with kids, I can easily select the proper age ranges for each ticket, so that I can make sure that each ticket is properly considered and charged as a child or adult.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Criterion 1**: Given that I am on the search page, when I select the number of adults and children that are travelling, then the system should properly display the total passenger count.
* **Criterion 2**: Given that I have opened the dropdown, when I am changing the adult and children count, then I should clearly see the age ranges that are considered child, adult or infant.
* **Criterion 3**: Given that I have opened the dropdown, when I am changing the adult and children count, then I should be able to either manually type the number or and increment/decrement button.
