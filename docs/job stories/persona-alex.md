### Context and Roles
**Name**: Alex
  * Alex is a university student who occasionally books flights for travel, holidays, or going home.
  * He usually uses his phone to search for flights and prefers a simple and fast system

### Goals and Success
**Primary goals**: Find affordable flights within his budget, compare flight options easily, and complete booking quickly.
  * He finds a flight that matches his budget and schedule.
  * He succeeds in his goal when he completes the booking without confusion or mistakes.

### Pain Points
* Too many flight options may make it hard to choose.
* Must be straightforward to compare prices and times quickly.
* The booking process needs to be clear.
* Extra costs must be clear.

### Constraints and Non-Goals
#### Constraints
* Limited budget as a student
* Limited time to search
* Mostly uses mobile devices

#### Non-Goals
* Not interested in luxury upgrades
* Does not care about loyalty programs
* Not looking for premium services

---

## User Stories

### 1. Flight Sorting Option
> **As a** student, 
> I can find affordable flights quickly, 
> so that I can save as much money as possible when travelling.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Criterion 1**: Given that I have searched for a flight, when I sort the results by the least expensive prices, then the least expensive flight for my destination should be the top result.
* **Criterion 2**: Given that I have searched for a flight, when the results are displayed, then I should be able to filter out expensive flights from the results.

### 2. Clear Flight Information
> **As a** customer, 
> I want to compare flight prices quickly,
> so that I can choose the best option for me.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Criterion 1**: Given that the flight results are displayed, when I am scrolling through the results, I can clearly differentiate the price and any discounts on each result.

### 3. Simple UI
> **As a** user of the website, 
> I want a simple user interface and a booking process, 
> so that I can navigate through the website easily and complete my booking quickly.
